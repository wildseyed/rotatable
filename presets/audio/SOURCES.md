# Sample sources & licenses

Everything in this folder ships with Rotatable and is used by the factory
presets. All files are 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit WAV.

## drums/ — synthesized (CC0)

`kick snare clap chh ohh tom cowbell clave` — synthesized with SoX
specifically for this project (2026-09-28). Public domain / CC0, no
attribution required.

## instruments/ — BBC Philharmonia Orchestra (CC BY 2.5)

`mandolin_B3 violin_A4 cello_G3 contrabass_G2 trumpet_As4 horn_G4 tuba_As2
oboe_F4 saxophone_C4` — single sustained notes from the BBC Philharmonia
free orchestral samples, via https://archive.org/details/orchestral_samples
(license: CC BY 2.5). Files were renamed by detected pitch (the archive.org
repack uses numbered files), trimmed to ≤3 s, downmixed to mono, and peak-
normalized. Credit: **BBC Philharmonia Orchestra**.

`theremin_Cs4` — from the Berklee College of Music sample collection for
OLPC (`Berklee44v16`, https://archive.org/details/Berklee44v16, license:
CC BY 3.0), trimmed/downmixed/normalized. Credit: **Berklee College of
Music**.

## animals/ — Red Library (CC0)

`elephant monkey bear frog crickets bee birds growl` — from "Red Library:
Animals Misc" (https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Animals_Misc,
license: CC0). Loudest 4 s window extracted, downmixed to mono,
peak-normalized. Note: `growl` is a human-performed animal growl (as named
in the source library).

## root folder — TR-808/909 one-shots (v1 factory kit)

`TR808-Clhh TR909-909_Sd TR909-Dark_Bd TR909-Rough_Sd TR909-Syn_Rim` —
classic drum-machine one-shots bundled since v1; used by the original
factory presets.
